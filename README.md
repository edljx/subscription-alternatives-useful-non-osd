### About edljx
We are a friendly, scrappy tech hub dedicated to helping curious builders replace expensive subscriptions with practical, affordable alternatives. We believe technology should empower, not bankrupt. Whether you're a student, indie creator, or just a tinkerer, we're here to share honest guides and real-world tools that respect your wallet.

### 📖 360° Overview
|**Why is this helpful?**  
While Open Source is ideal, sometimes the best tool for the job is proprietary. This repository acknowledges that pragmatism beats purism. These tools are the industry standards that professionals rely on.

|**Who is this for?**  
Professionals who need 'it just works' reliability and support, even if it costs money or is closed source.

### 🌍 Impact on the Everyday Person & Tech Enthusiast
|**For the Everyday Person:**  
This isn't just "computer code"; it's about autonomy. By understanding and using these tools, you stop being a passive consumer who pays monthly rent for digital life. You become an owner. It means keeping your family photos safe without paying Apple/Google, working from anywhere without restriction, or learning a skill that can double your income.

|**For the Tech Enthusiast:**  
This is your playground. These resources remove the guardrails found in commercial software. You get to see "how the sausage is made," tweak every config file, and build systems that are faster, more private, and uniquely yours. It's the difference between driving an automatic sedan and tuning a manual racecar.

|**How to use this safely:**  
Be mindful of data privacy policies. Closed source software cannot be audited by the community.

### ✨ Specific Benefits to Your Life
Best-in-class user experience (UX) and often better integration with corporate ecosystems.

### About This Repository
This repository contains software alternatives that are **useful and effective**, but do **not** strictly meet the [Open Source Definition (OSD)](https://opensource.org/osd) (e.g., they might be freemium or source-available).

For fully open-source alternatives, please see our main repository: **[foss-subscription-alternatives](../foss-subscription-alternatives)**.

### Design & Creative Tools
*   **[DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve)** - Professional video editing and color grading. *Note: Free version available, not open source.*
*   **[Figma](https://www.figma.com)** - Browser-based collaborative UI design with real-time multiplayer editing. *Note: Free tier; proprietary.*
*   **[Canva](https://www.canva.com)** - Template-driven graphic design for social media, presentations, and print. *Note: Freemium; proprietary.*
*   **[Adobe Photoshop](https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop.html)** - Industry-standard raster image editor with neural filters. *Note: Subscription, proprietary.*
*   **[Adobe Illustrator](https://www.adobe.com/products/illustrator.html)** - Vector graphics editor for logos, icons, and typography. *Note: Subscription, proprietary.*
*   **[Affinity Designer](https://affinity.serif.com/designer/)** - One-time-purchase vector and raster design app. *Note: Paid, proprietary.*

*   **[Sketch](https://www.sketch.com)** - macOS UI design app with shared libraries and prototyping. *Note: Subscription, proprietary.*
*   **[Pixelmator Pro](https://www.pixelmator.com/pro/)** - macOS-native image editor with ML-powered tools. *Note: One-time purchase, proprietary.*
